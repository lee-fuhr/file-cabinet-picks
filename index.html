How we choose our picks

We rely on a combination of long-term owner feedback from forums and retailer reviews, along with published expert assessments from office-furniture specialists. The things that actually break, sticky drawer slides, misaligned locks, bent suspension rails, matter more than glossy marketing specs. Each pick is cross-checked against dozens of user reports that cover one, three, and even five years of use. If a cabinet develops a wobble, a lock jams, or the paint chips easily, it’s out. We also weigh reputation for customer support and parts availability, because even a great product is only as good as the company behind it.
