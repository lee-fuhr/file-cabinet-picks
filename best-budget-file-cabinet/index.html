A reliable file cabinet is one of those home-office purchases you rarely think about until you need it. A good one keeps your papers organized, locks securely, and doesn’t wobble when you open the top drawer. But budget cabinets often cut corners on drawer glides and metal gauge, so it’s worth knowing which features matter most before you buy. When choosing a budget file cabinet, focus on how the drawers feel (ball-bearing slides are a plus), the quality of the lock (look for a central locking bar, not a cheap cam lock), and the overall weight. Heavier cabinets tend to be sturdier but harder to move. Measure your space, think about whether you need letter or legal size, and decide if you want a vertical or lateral style.
